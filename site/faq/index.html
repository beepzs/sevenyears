About The Series

What is The First Seven Years? ▾

The First Seven Years is a multi-media knowledge library and podcast hosted by Dr. Murray Clarke and Bryce Wylde. It focuses on the crucial developmental years from age 0 to 7, exploring nutrition, gut health, sleep, development, and pediatric wellness.

Clinical & Medical

Is this content a substitute for pediatric medical care? ▾

No. All content on The First Seven Years is educational and informative. It does not constitute individual medical advice or diagnosis. Always consult your qualified pediatrician or family doctor for your child’s specific symptoms.

Ask the Doctor

How does Dr. Murray choose which questions to answer? ▾

Dr. Murray and the clinical team review every inbound parent question. Questions addressing common challenges or emerging topics are turned into parent answers and podcast segments for the whole community.

The Book

When is Dr. Murray Clarke’s upcoming book being released? ▾

The book is currently in production for release in 2027. You can join the priority waitlist on our book page to receive early sample chapters and launch updates.

Topics & Content

Why is so much emphasis placed on the gut microbiome? ▾

Up to 70% of a child’s immune system is trained in the gut. Modern research reveals deep connections between the early microbiome and childhood allergies, eczema, mood, and cognitive development.
