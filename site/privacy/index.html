Privacy Policy

Transparent privacy policy complying with global health and children privacy standards.

We respect family privacy. Submissions made through Ask the Doctor are kept confidential and de-identified before educational publishing.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in radical data minimization. When you interact with our platform, the only information collected is:

  • Email Addresses: If you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter or submit an inquiry via Ask the Doctor.
  • Parent Inquiries: Questions submitted to our pediatric clinical panel. All questions are de-identified before discussion.
  • Technical Analytics: Privacy-friendly aggregate usage metrics to understand which topics are most helpful.

2. Children’s Online Privacy (COPPA & Health Regulations)

Our service is designed exclusively for adult parents, guardians, and medical practitioners. We never knowingly collect personal contact information from children under the age of 13.

3. Video Embedding & YouTube Facade

All videos on The First Seven Years load using an interactive privacy facade. No external tracking cookies or scripts are loaded from YouTube or third parties until you actively click the play button.

4. Health Information Confidentiality

Inquiries sent through Ask the Doctor are treated as confidential editorial research. They do not create an electronic health record (EHR) or patient chart. Please do not submit emergency medical information through online forms.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You may request removal of your email or submitted question at any time by contacting us at hello@thefirstsevenyears.com.
