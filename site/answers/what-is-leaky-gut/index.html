Gut health & microbiome

What is “leaky gut”, really?

E
Dr. Elisa SongGuest · Episode 26 · Integrative pediatrician
Published 30 Sep 2026 Reviewed 24 Sep 2026 Pending clinical review

The short answer

“Leaky gut” describes a gut lining that lets more through than it should — researchers call it increased intestinal permeability. It is measurable in studies, but it is not a diagnosis on its own. Think of it as one way gut health may connect to allergies, skin and mood.

Watch the full answer

Dr. Elisa Song answers this in the video above, drawn from Episode 26 of The First Seven Years. For the whole discussion, watch the full episode.

When to talk to your doctor

If you are worried about your child, if symptoms are getting worse, or if something just doesn’t feel right, speak to your pediatrician. Trust your instincts — you know your child best.
