Are pets and a bit of dirt actually good for my child?

Dr. Elisa SongGuest · Episode 26 · Integrative pediatrician
Published 6 Sep 2026 Reviewed 21 Sep 2026 Pending clinical review

The short answer

Often, yes. Many studies link early, everyday exposure — pets, outdoor play, older siblings — to lower rates of allergy and asthma. It is sometimes called the “old friends” idea. It does not replace handwashing before meals and after the toilet.

When to talk to your doctor

If you are worried about your child, if symptoms are getting worse, or if something just doesn’t feel right, speak to your pediatrician. Trust your instincts — you know your child best.
