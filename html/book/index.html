Inside the book

Seven chapters, one for each year — plus a chapter for before day one.

  1. Before day one

    Preconception, pregnancy and the microbiome your baby inherits.

  2. Year one

    Feeding, skin, sleep and an immune system learning fast.

  3. Years two and three

    First foods become picky eating; first colds become nursery bugs.

  4. Years four to seven

    Focus, feelings, school-day food and a foundation for life.

Honest, expert answers for parents of children aged 0 to 7 — from the doctors and researchers on The First Seven Years.
